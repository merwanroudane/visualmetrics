# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project exists to help people understand difficult ideas. Everyone should
be able to take part without harassment, regardless of experience, background,
language, nationality, disability, age, gender, religion or any other personal
characteristic.

## Expected behaviour

- Be patient with questions. A question that seems basic is usually a place
  where an explanation was unclear, which is useful information for a teaching
  project.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept that reviewers may disagree with you; the science is settled by
  argument and evidence, not by seniority.
- Write in whichever of English, Arabic or French you are comfortable with.
  Issues and pull requests are welcome in all three.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Dismissing a contribution because of who wrote it, where they studied or
  which language they write in.

## Scientific conduct

This project makes claims about mathematics and statistics, so two further
things count as misconduct here:

- **Knowingly overstating evidence.** Presenting a simulation as a proof, or
  removing a stated limitation to make a result look stronger.
- **Refusing to correct a mistake.** Everyone gets things wrong. Leaving a
  known error in place, once it has been demonstrated, is the problem.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **merwanroudane920@gmail.com**. Reports are
handled confidentially and reviewed as quickly as possible.

Consequences range from a private warning through a temporary ban to permanent
exclusion from the project, depending on severity and repetition. The
maintainer will explain the reason for any action taken.
